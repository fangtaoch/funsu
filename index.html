Home / Quality Control

Quality Control You Can Trust

We inspect every order before it ships, so you receive exactly what you ordered.

Inspection Process

Four Steps, Zero Surprises

Pre-Inspection Preparation

We define AQL standards, prepare checklists, and confirm specifications with the factory.

During Production

We monitor key stages and request live video updates. You can join remotely.

Final Inspection

Random sampling, functional testing, and packaging checks. We use calibrated tools.

Third-Party Inspection

For critical orders, we arrange SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas at ~$100 per inspection.

Live Video Inspections

We conduct live video walkthroughs of the production line and warehouse. You can ask to inspect specific cartons or units in real time. No pre-recorded footage.

Detailed Reports with Evidence

Every inspection includes time-stamped photos, signed checklists, and a full report. We keep samples for your reference.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask

What is your defect rate?

We aim for <1% and work with factories to correct any issues before shipment.

Can I use my own inspection agency?

Absolutely. We coordinate with any third-party you choose.

Company Story

Why We Started Funsu

After years of working in pet product manufacturing and export, we saw a gap: small and medium buyers struggled to find reliable factories with flexible MOQ and professional quality control.

Funsu was founded to bridge that gap. We don't own factories; we manage them. Our role is to be your eyes and ears on the ground, ensuring every order is smooth and every product meets your standards.
